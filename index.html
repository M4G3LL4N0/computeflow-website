Compute control plane · not a GPU marketplace

Terafab builds compute. ComputeFlow decides where a job should run.

Teams still pick a vendor in a spreadsheet, then discover latency, energy, and sovereignty after the bill arrives. ComputeFlow takes a workload definition and returns a weighted split across pools — cloud GPU, private cluster, sovereign region, edge, and reserved future supply — with the trade-offs written in plain English.
